Why it matters

Two conditions drive most of your absence.

Cardiometabolic disease builds silently for years before it presents. Musculoskeletal pain resolves slowly when people sit in referral queues. Both are measurable, both respond to early intervention — and both are usually invisible to an employer until someone is signed off.

We bring the clinic to your site, find what is there, and treat it in the same visit where we can.

Found earlier
Risk identified while it is still reversible, not after an event.
Treated faster
Diagnosis and treatment in one appointment, at your site, with no referral wait.
Measured after
Worker-reported outcomes at 1, 3, 6 and 12 months through our Insyta portal.
Reported back
Anonymised, GDPR-compliant aggregate reporting your board can act on.
