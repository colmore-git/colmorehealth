Fall Recovery Assessment

Slips, Trips & Falls Prevention

Most falls programmes stop at hazard signage. Ours measures the person: who has lost the strength and balance to recover a stumble, and what to do about it before they go down.

The pathway

Identify, rehabilitate, monitor.

01
Measure
Dynamometry gives an objective strength reading rather than a subjective impression.
02
Assess
Clinical assessment and questionnaire identify those with low fall recovery capacity.
03
Rehabilitate
Targeted strength and balance work for those flagged as at risk.
04
Monitor
Repeat measures show whether capacity is actually improving.

Why fall recovery, not just fall risk.

Everybody stumbles. What separates a near miss from a reportable injury is whether the person has the strength and reaction to catch themselves. That capacity is measurable — and it is trainable.

For an ageing workforce, or one working at height, on uneven ground or around moving plant, it is one of the few injury risks you can genuinely reduce at the individual level.
