Health screening

Find the risk while it is still reversible.

Cardiometabolic disease builds silently for years. Onsite screening gives every employee their own numbers — and gives you a clear picture of where the risk sits.

Tiered checks, built around the time you have.

Screening runs in tiers, from a quick baseline check through to a full cardiometabolic and cardiac profile. Every tier is delivered onsite by a clinician, and every employee has their results explained to them in plain English before they leave the room.

Blood pressure, height and weight carry through every tier, so trends stay comparable however you scale the programme up or down.

Cardiac screening

Blood pressure alone does not show what the heart is doing.

Silent structural change

Sustained high blood pressure thickens the heart muscle long before symptoms appear. An ECG picks up what a cuff reading cannot.

Rhythm and conduction

Arrhythmias and conduction abnormalities are often the first sign that something needs investigating properly.

A clear escalation route

Anything abnormal is explained on the day, with a written recommendation the employee can take straight to their GP.

We do not just check. We keep measuring.

Results flow into Insyta, our outcomes portal: a personalised cardiovascular risk profile and lifestyle guidance for each employee, worker-reported outcome measures at 1, 3, 6 and 12 months, and anonymised aggregate reporting for you.
