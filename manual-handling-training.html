Manual Handling Training

Practical, task-specific technique training delivered onsite by our clinicians — built around the lifts, carries and awkward loads your teams actually handle.

Training that reflects the real task.

Generic manual handling courses teach a textbook lift that rarely matches the job. Ours is delivered by clinicians who assess and treat these injuries every week, and who build the session around your own tasks, loads and working environment.

Because the same clinician can assess and treat on site, anyone who reports pain during the session can be seen properly rather than told to book an appointment.

A session typically covers
›
How load, posture and repetition combine to cause injury
›
Technique practice using your own equipment and loads
›
Team handling and awkward or restricted-space lifts
›
Recognising early warning signs and what to do about them
›
When to stop, report and seek assessment
