Advanced DSE Assessment

Clinician-led workstation assessment for symptomatic or complex cases — where a standard self-assessment has been completed and the problem has not gone away.

When the checklist has not solved it.

Standard DSE self-assessments catch the obvious. They do not explain why one person still has neck pain in a perfectly compliant setup. A Tier 2 assessment starts with the person rather than the desk: what hurts, when, and what the body is doing to compensate.

You get a written set of corrective recommendations, and where symptoms need treating, a clinical plan rather than a furniture order.

The assessment includes
›
Symptom history and clinical screening of the affected area
›
Postural and movement assessment at the actual workstation
›
Equipment, layout and setup adjustments made on the spot
›
Written recommendations for you and for the employee
›
Onward treatment or rehab plan where it is needed
Use it for referrals

The escalation route when a self-assessment flags something you cannot resolve internally.

Onsite or virtual

Delivered at the desk, or remotely for home-based and hybrid workers.

Evidence for the file

A clinician's written assessment and recommendations you can act on and record.

Refer a complex case, or set up a standing route.
